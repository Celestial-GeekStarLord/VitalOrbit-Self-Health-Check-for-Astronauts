Reference numbers

  • NASA's career limit for spaceflight radiation is under 600 mSv, the same for all ages and sexes. The limit for one solar particle event is 250 mSv. (NASA-STD-3001, NASA technical brief)
  • A six-month stay on the ISS exposes an astronaut to roughly 50 to 120 mSv, and Earth's natural background gives about 3 mSv per year. (Space.com, citing the National Academies report)
  • Neutrons make up about 30% of total radiation on the ISS. (CSA)

Space weather data comes from NASA's DONKI database through the NASA Open APIs. The summary above is a simple count of notifications from the last 72 hours, not a forecast. Your mission's radiation team decides shelter actions.
